WhatsApp Business and SMS launch guide

Step 1. Pick one journey

  • Start with a single high-volume journey: payment reminders, delivery updates, appointment confirmations or OTPs.
  • Write down what success looks like: fewer calls, faster replies or higher completion.

Step 2. Decide the job of each channel

  • SMS: OTPs, alerts and short updates that must reach every phone.
  • WhatsApp: richer conversations with images, buttons, order updates, support and two-way chat.

Step 3. Get SMS ready

  • Register your sender names with the operators or regulator in each country you send to. Unregistered senders are commonly blocked.
  • Separate promotional and transactional traffic, and respect local sending hours for promotions.

Step 4. Set up WhatsApp Business

  • Verify your business with Meta and connect a dedicated number.
  • Apply for the verified badge: a consistent brand name, an active website and public presence help the review.
  • Submit message templates for approval, in Arabic and English, with variables for personalisation.
  • Collect and record opt-in before the first business-initiated message.

Step 5. Automate, then hand over

  • Let the chatbot answer the five most common questions.
  • Pass the conversation to a live agent with its history when the customer asks, or when the bot cannot help.

Step 6. Integrate

  • Trigger messages from your CRM, app or order system by API.
  • Show the conversation inside the contact-centre desktop so agents see one history.

Step 7. Peak-day readiness

  • Campaign templates approved at least a week ahead.
  • Expected peak volume shared with your provider.
  • Fallback: if a WhatsApp message is not delivered, send the key update by SMS.

Measure

Track delivery rate, read rate, replies and completions per template. Retire weak templates and test new ones each month.

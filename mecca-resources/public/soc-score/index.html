What's your 24x7 security readiness score?

10 yes/no questions. Get a score out of 100 and a personal fix list for every gap. Useful before your next audit or board review.

1. Is someone watching security alerts 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and public holidays?

2. Do you know how long it takes, on average, from an alert to a first response?

3. Are cloud workloads monitored to the same standard as on-premises systems?

4. Is multi-factor authentication enforced for every administrator and remote user?

5. Is there a current list of who has privileged access, reviewed in the last 6 months?

6. Have you run an incident-response exercise in the last 12 months?

7. Do you receive threat intelligence specific to your sector and region?

8. Can you produce evidence for your regulator or auditor within a week?

9. Are critical systems patched within a defined, measured time?

10. Is at least one backup copy isolated so ransomware cannot reach it?
